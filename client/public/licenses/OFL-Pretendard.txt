Pretendard
==========

CC on Browser bundles four Pretendard web fonts:
  client/src/fonts/Pretendard-{Regular,Medium,SemiBold,Bold}.woff2
and ships them in the published package as client/dist/assets/Pretendard-*.woff2.

These files are byte-for-byte identical to the upstream Pretendard v1.3.9 release
(Pretendard-1.3.9.zip -> web/static/woff2/), verified by SHA-256 on 2026-09-04:

  fad853f7f47c6c8b103171e7193fa095708cdcd70850a71d93aa5379e8a61d63  Pretendard-Regular.woff2
  d03481330eeba0659ab5b87f25ceb504a35de377dd90a0d0aba2982eb2d05e2c  Pretendard-Medium.woff2
  c863f76a7de5c1ddc1ed8b2fa794964530774592c4f31407a84e2a2ae93f17f0  Pretendard-SemiBold.woff2
  4609c3356e536fafe38f4add0daeceb3d8595d3057bce13c428c33ddbd43d362  Pretendard-Bold.woff2
