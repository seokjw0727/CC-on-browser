Monoplex KR
===========

CC on Browser bundles two Monoplex KR web fonts:
  client/src/fonts/MonoplexKR-{Regular,Bold}.woff2
and ships them in the published package as client/dist/assets/MonoplexKR-*.woff2.

Upstream release v0.0.2 distributes TrueType (.ttf) only, so these .woff2 files were
produced from the upstream TTFs by WOFF2 compression. Per SIL OFL FAQ 2.2.1, a WOFF
conversion may keep the Reserved Font Name when the original font data is unchanged
apart from the compression and WOFF-specific metadata is either omitted altogether or
preserved intact.

Both conditions were checked byte-for-byte against the upstream v0.0.2 TTFs (verified
2026-09-04, for both Regular and Bold):

  * Same 17 tables, no additions and no removals.
  * 14 of the 17 are byte-identical to the upstream TTF, including the full name table:
      name[0] Copyright  "Copyright (c) 2021, Kim Yangsu"
      name[1] Family     "Monoplex KR"
      name[4] Full name  "Monoplex KR Regular" / "Monoplex KR Bold"
      name[6] PostScript "MonoplexKR-Regular" / "MonoplexKR-Bold"
  * glyf and loca are re-encoded, which the WOFF2 specification mandates as part of the
    compression itself; no glyph outlines were edited, subset or re-hinted.
  * head differs in exactly two places: checkSumAdjustment, which is recomputed whenever
    an sfnt is reassembled, and bit 11 of head.flags (0x0800), which the OpenType
    specification defines as "font data has been converted to a lossless compressed
    format" and which the converter is expected to set. Every other head field --
    unitsPerEm (1000), macStyle, indexToLocFormat, fontRevision, the bounding box and
    the created/modified dates -- is unchanged.
  * No WOFF metadata or private block is present (metaLength = 0, privLength = 0).

The Reserved Font Names above are therefore retained legitimately. Upstream:
https://github.com/y-kim/monoplex
